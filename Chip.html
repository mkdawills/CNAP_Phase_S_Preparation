Helpful notes
Page limits
  • Specific Aims: 1 page.
  • Research Strategy: 6 pages.
  • Project Summary/Abstract: 30 lines of text.
  • O&M Plan input: becomes part of the Center Research Strategy, which has 12 pages for Sections A–E combined.
The order of the work
  • Aims first. Your Specific Aims (due Fri, Nov 13) should follow the frame of the Center's Specific Aims, which Heather shares with all Core Directors Fri, Nov 6.
  • Strategy due before budget. The Research Strategy is due Mon, Nov 30, and the Budget and Budget Justification are ready for Roxana's review Fri, Dec 4. The budget costs out what the strategy describes.
Reviews before submission
  • Budget review: Roxana (CNAP's budget reviewer) first, then Tim in Central Office. Your revised (final) budget is ready for her review the morning of Fri, Jan 15.
  • Outside scientific review: your Specific Aims and Research Strategy go to an external reviewer Mon, Dec 7. Revisions are due Mon, Jan 11.
  • Biosketch review: Kim in Central Office reviews your draft (ready for her review Thu, Dec 17). Your final is due Wed, Jan 13.
Phase S rules for your core (NOFO)
  • Phase S sustains, modernizes, and consolidates the cores CNAP already has.
  • No duplicate services, and open your services to others. The core can't duplicate services already at K-State, and it must be available to other K-State investigators and to other IDeA-state institutions.
  • Serve the Scientific Research Program (SRP) Core. The core supports the projects led by SRP Investigators (SIs). Reviewers look for how it meets the SRP Core's needs.
  • Budget: five years; no Alterations & Renovations allowed in a Research Core.
What doesn't go in your core (NOFO)
  • Letters of support. All support goes in the single Institutional Letter in the Center (Overall).
  • Data Management and Sharing Plan. It goes in the Center (Overall) only.
  • Project Narrative. Center (Overall) only.
  • Appendix: optional and limited to specific item types; most applications include none. If your core needs one, tell Morgan by Fri, Dec 11 (CNAP).

Requirements

  • 1 page. Follow NIH's standard Specific Aims instructions; the NOFO adds nothing specific for core Aims.
  • Existing core only. No new cores may be established in Phase S. Your aims describe sustaining, modernizing, or consolidating the core CNAP already has.

How reviewers will score it

Reviewers score your core with five questions:

  • Is the request to support the core well justified?
  • Does the core give suitable scientific support to the Center's research area?
  • Does the business plan account for future growth in the core's user base and for the core's sustainability (the Phase S question)?
  • Does the Core Director have the expertise to oversee maintaining and growing the core's resources and facilities?
  • Are there enough institutional commitments to keep the resources and facilities the core needs available?
For reference: the NOFO's exact words
Review criteria · NOFO, Sec. V.1, Review Criteria for Cores › Research Cores

Is the request to support the Research Core well justified? Does the Core provide suitable scientific support to the proposed scientific area of the COBRE? Does the business plan account for future growth in the Core's user base ensuring an expansion of its reach (COBRE-E) and its sustainability (COBRE-S), as applicable? Does the Core Director have the expertise to provide the necessary oversight to administer the maintenance and growth of existing core resources and laboratory facilities to carry out the scientific objectives outlined in the application? Are there sufficient institutional commitments to ensure that the resources and facilities required to sustain and enhance the Core will be available?

MonNov 30 3. BN Core Research StrategyThe main description of the BN Core: what it provides, who uses it, and how it will be sustained. 6 pages

What to write (NOFO requirements, in plain language)

  • Explain how the core supports the Center's science. Describe which areas of the Center's research the core serves and how investigators use it. Then name related research resources already at K-State (for example, other cores or shared instruments) and explain how your core fits with them.
  • Spell out what the core provides. List the specific technologies, instruments, and services an investigator can get from the core.
  • Justify any equipment or facility changes. For each new instrument, upgrade, or change to the core's facilities, explain why it's needed. Show that you've accounted for the infrastructure and services K-State already has that relate to the request.
  • Show the core's value to the Center. Explain how the core expands research, how its services meet the needs of the SRP Core (the projects led by SRP Investigators), and how the core will be sustained.
  • Describe who uses the core. Say who uses it now (for example, which labs, departments, or institutions) and how you plan to grow that user base.
  • Lay out the business plan. Explain how you decide which service requests come first, what users are charged (the fee structure), and what it costs to maintain the core.

Restrictions

  • 6 pages.
  • No new cores in Phase S. Describe the existing core being sustained, modernized, or consolidated.
  • No duplicate services. A core that duplicates services already available at K-State isn't allowed.
  • Open to others. The core must be available to other K-State investigators and to investigators at other IDeA-state institutions.

Encouraged by the NOFO

  • Building on what exists: using, modifying, or expanding existing core resources supported by institutional funds or other IDeA programs.
  • Consolidation and sharing: consolidating existing cores (for technology advancement or administrative efficiency) and sharing facilities supported by other IDeA programs (INBRE, CTR, and other COBREs).

CNAP guidance (not NOFO rules)

  • Write the strategy before the budget. The budget (due Fri, Dec 4) costs out what the strategy describes. A budget line with no matching activity here will be flagged.
  • Frame growth around sustainability. Growing the user base or services isn't prohibited; tie it to keeping the core running after COBRE support ends.
  • Answer the five review questions directly, especially the business plan and institutional commitments.

How reviewers will score it

  • Is the request to support the core well justified?
  • Does the core give suitable scientific support to the Center's research area?
  • Does the business plan account for future growth in the core's user base and for the core's sustainability (the Phase S question)?
  • Does the Core Director have the expertise to oversee maintaining and growing the core's resources and facilities?
  • Are there enough institutional commitments to keep the resources and facilities the core needs available?
For reference: the NOFO's exact words
NOFO, Sec. IV.2, Research Core › PHS 398 Research Plan › Research Strategy

Research Strategy: Describe and justify:

  1. How the Core's resources will be used to support the scientific breadth of the Center. Applicants should also describe other related research resources already available at the applicant organization,
  2. The technology, instrumentation, and/or services the Core will provide,
  3. Instruments, equipment or modification of existing core facilities. Account for the organization's current infrastructure and offered services pertinent to the request,
  4. The contribution of the Core as it expands research, the service it would provide to meet the needs of the SRP, and its sustainability,
  5. The Core's user base and any plans for its expansion, and
  6. The business plan for operation of the Core, including prioritization of service requests, user fee structure, and maintenance costs.
NOFO, Sec. IV.2, Research Core (component preamble)

Research Cores should be made available to other investigators at the institution as well as those from other IDeA state institutions. Cores that duplicate services already available at the applicant institution are not allowed. Utilization, modification, or expansion of existing Core resources supported by institutional funds or other IDeA programs to accomplish the goals of proposed COBRE research is strongly encouraged.

NOFO, Sec. I (Research Core(s), optional)

No new cores may be established in the COBRE-S phase. The consolidation of existing Cores for technology advancement and/or administrative efficiency and the sharing of research facilities supported by other IDeA programs (e.g., INBRE, CTR, and other COBREs) is strongly encouraged.

FriDec 4 4. BN Core Budget + Budget JustificationThe BN Core's five-year budget (entered as forms) and the written justification for each cost. Form entries + justificationRoxana (reviews)

Requirements

  • Five years. A detailed budget for the entire project period.
  • Justification. The NOFO adds no special justification instructions, so NIH's standard (SF424 R&R) instructions apply.

What a Research Core can pay for

  • Personnel and supplies.
  • New equipment and modernizing instruments.
  • Using, modifying, or expanding existing resources needed for the Center's scientific goals.

Restrictions

  • No Alterations & Renovations (A&R) in a Research Core.
  • No new cores. The NOFO's budget text is shared with Phase E; its wording about establishing Research Core facilities applies only to Phase E.
  • Total cap. All of the Center's budgets together must not exceed $1.5 million in direct costs per year (consortium F&A excluded). Roxana tracks the total.

CNAP guidance (not NOFO rules)

  • Every cost traces to the Research Strategy. Each budget line should match an activity the strategy describes.
  • Frame costs for Phase S: sustaining, modernizing, or consolidating the existing core.
  • Justify each year: personnel, supplies, equipment purchases or modernization, and core operations.

Review timeline (CNAP)

  • Fri, Dec 4: Budget and Budget Justification in WORKING DRAFTS, with Progress set to Ready for Review in your Smartsheet row. Roxana reviews them Dec 7–11 and sends any fixes back to you.
  • Mon, Dec 14: Roxana sends every budget to Tim in Central Office. Tim reviews for at least two working weeks and may flag issues as he finds them; fix each one as it reaches you.
  • Fri, Jan 8: All of Tim's comments are back.
  • Fri, Jan 15 (morning): Your revised (final) budget and justification in WORKING DRAFTS, with Progress set to Ready for Review in your Smartsheet row. Roxana checks them and sends the final budgets to Tim that day. After this, critical edits only.
  • Tue, Jan 19: Morgan uploads the final budgets to Cayuse (K-State is closed Mon, Jan 18).

How reviewers will score it

  • Reviewers consider whether the budget and the requested period of support are fully justified and reasonable for the proposed research.
For reference: the NOFO's exact words
NOFO, Sec. IV.2, Research Core › Budget (Research Core)

Funds may be requested to establish Research Core facilities and/or to use, modify, or expand existing resources that are necessary for accomplishing the scientific goals of the Center. In addition to personnel and supply costs, the acquisition of new equipment and modernization of instrumentation may be proposed. Alteration and Renovation (A&R) of core facilities cannot be proposed under this component.

Core facilities should have a detailed budget for the entire proposed project period (five years).

Review criteria · NOFO, Sec. V.1, Additional Review Considerations - Overall › Budget and Period of Support

Reviewers will consider whether the budget and the requested period of support are fully justified and reasonable in relation to the proposed research.
