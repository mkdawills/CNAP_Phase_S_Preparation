Executive summary: please read first

  • Smartsheet is our central project-management hub.
  • All drafts should be saved in the Dropbox WORKING DRAFTS folder.
  • The FINAL VERSIONS folder is reserved for approved, submission-ready documents.
  • Coordinate with other contributors when your component overlaps with their work.

How a document moves

The same path for every deliverable: write in WORKING DRAFTS, mark it Ready for Review, apply feedback, and let Morgan Wills and Heather Bailey finalize it.

1 · StartCreate the documentStart with the applicable NIH-provided form or document, if required. Otherwise, create a Word document using the applicable component instructions and formatting guidance.Status: Not Started → In ProgressFile lives in: WORKING DRAFTS (once you save it)
2 · WriteSave in WORKING DRAFTSAll writing and revisions take place in your component's WORKING DRAFTS folder. Save your working draft there by the internal due date.Status: In ProgressFile lives in: WORKING DRAFTS
3 · ReviewSet Ready for ReviewSetting Ready for Review in Smartsheet tells your reviewer the draft is waiting: internal (Roxana, Tim, or Kim) or the identified external scientific reviewer.Status: Ready for ReviewFile lives in: WORKING DRAFTS
4 · ReviseApply feedbackIncorporate the reviewers' edits in WORKING DRAFTS.Status: Applying Reviewer FeedbackFile lives in: WORKING DRAFTS
5 · FinalizeTell Morgan and HeatherThey give it a final review, move it to the FINAL VERSIONS folder for you, and change its status to Finalized. You don't need to move the file or update the status yourself.Status: FinalizedFile lives in: FINAL VERSIONS (moved by Morgan and Heather)
Important: Completing a draft does not automatically mean that a component is approved for submission. Only documents that have completed the required review and approval process should be placed in the FINAL VERSIONS folder.

Smartsheet

Why Smartsheet

Smartsheet is the live version of every packet. Dates, links, and assignments are updated there first, so if Smartsheet and a packet (or this site) ever differ, Smartsheet wins.

Open Smartsheet

How to use it

  • Find your rows: filter by your name in the Assigned To column.
  • Open what you need from your row: your directions (your packet), your WORKING DRAFTS folder, your FINAL VERSIONS folder, and reference materials such as prior COBRE proposals.
  • Update your status from the Status drop-down as you work. Setting Ready for Review tells your reviewer the draft is waiting.
  • Due date definition: save your working draft in your WORKING DRAFTS folder by the internal due date.
  • Add the deadlines to your calendar: subscribe to the Smartsheet calendar in Outlook or Google Calendar, and date changes update automatically.
  • Getting in: accept the invitation email and sign in with your K-State account. The mobile app works too.

Please keep your assigned Smartsheet status up to date so the Admin Core can monitor progress, coordinate reviews, and identify potential delays.

Reviewers

The CNAP Admin Core will identify the designated reviewer(s) for each document and let you know who they are. Please feel free to suggest reviewers to the Admin Core.

When your document is final

Let Morgan Wills and Heather Bailey know. They will give it a final review, move it to the FINAL VERSIONS folder for you, and change its status in Smartsheet to Finalized. You don't need to move the file or update the status yourself.

Working drafts: when creating a document

  1. Start with the applicable NIH-provided form or document, if required.
  2. Otherwise, create a Word document using the applicable component instructions and formatting guidance.
  3. Save the document in the designated WORKING DRAFTS folder.

When developing your component, consider how it supports the broader COBRE Phase S proposal. Where appropriate, coordinate with relevant contributors to ensure that the scientific narrative, core activities, milestones, evaluation measures, and resource requests align across the application.
