What PAR-27-055 asks about K-State’s strategic priorities, and what K-State’s strategic plan says.

PAR-27-055 asks about K-State’s strategic plan in eight places. All wording below is quoted from the NOFO.

Where in the applicationPAR-27-055 wording

Each row pairs a Next-Gen K-State quote with the related COBRE-S requirement. The pairings are suggestions for writers, not NIH language.

Next-Gen K-StateRelated COBRE-S requirement
“K-State will invest in consolidated core research facilities and associated staff — including the Biosecurity Research Institute, or BRI, Biomedical Core Facility, Materials Characterization Core, Biomanufacturing Core and Training Facility, and Integrated Genomics Facility — with dedicated operating, equipment and renovation funds…” (p. 29)Research Cores: COBRE-S strongly encourages consolidating existing cores and sharing facilities supported by other IDeA programs. Sustainability Plan and institutional letter: institutional resources and commitment to sustain the Center.
“Establish consolidated core research facilities with shared instrumentation to give faculty, students and industry partners shared access to sophisticated equipment and expert staff…” (p. 30)Research Cores: Cores should be made available to other investigators at the institution as well as those from other IDeA state institutions.

“Early-career faculty will be supported in their disciplinary development so they can become future research leaders…” (p. 28) and “Expand support and mentorship for early-career faculty in their disciplinary development…” (p. 31)SRP and Career Development Plan: COBRE-S applicants show how they will “further guide early career investigators towards independence”; each SI is assigned a mentor.

4 · Notes for Writers

Points to keep the alignment accurate and consistent across components.

  • Next-Gen K-State does not name neuroscience among its Opportunity Agenda focus areas or its named cluster-hire areas. The clearest connections are the research-enterprise priorities in Section 3: core facilities, early-career faculty, faculty hiring and research growth.
  • “Community health and well-being” is the Opportunity Agenda’s health-related focus area. Whether CNAP’s work is framed under it is a decision for Dr. Heather Bailey and the author of the institutional letter.
  • The institutional letter, the Overall Justification, and the SRP should point to the same plan priorities, in the same words.
  • Refer to the plan by its name, Next-Gen K-State, and quote it directly rather than paraphrasing its targets.
