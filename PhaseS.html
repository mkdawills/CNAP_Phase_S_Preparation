Phase S Overview & NOFO Comparisons

What Phase S is, what changed from earlier NOFOs, and who's eligible.
The SRP Core, review criteria, and key-term definitions now have their own pages.
This briefing covers PAR-27-055, the COBRE-E/S NOFO.
CNAP is applying for the PAR-27-055 Phase S (Sustainability) Phase.

Read the full NOFO (PAR-27-055) NIGMS PAR-27-055 FAQs
Max annual direct costs$1.5M
Duration5 years
Number of awardsUndetermined
Next due dateJan 25, 2027

Key changes in brief
  • No new cores. Consolidating existing cores, and sharing facilities supported by other IDeA programs, is strongly encouraged.
  • Scientific Research Program (SRP) is a required component. It now also coordinates mentoring and career development.
  • The External Advisory Committee (EAC) is now the Advisory Committee (AC).
  • Clinical trials are not allowed. Human subjects activities that are not clinical trials are allowed.
  • The Data Management and Sharing (DMS) Plan goes in the Overall component.
  • New SIs, starting research: without human subjects or vertebrate animals, notify NIGMS 14+ days ahead (no prior approval needed). With human subjects or vertebrate animals, NIGMS prior approval is required, submitted through the eRA Prior Approval module 30+ days ahead (NOT-OD-26-026).

NOFO numbers used in this briefing
  • PAR-27-055: COBRE-E/S NOFO (Expansion and Sustainability). This application (S phase). Current; expires May 26, 2029.
  • PAR-23-063: previous COBRE Phase 2 NOFO; reissued as PAR-27-055.
  • PAR-23-216: previous COBRE Phase 3 (Transitional Centers) NOFO; expired October 1, 2025. Compared in Section 3.
  • NOT-OD-26-026: NIH notice (not a NOFO) on prior approval requests through the eRA Prior Approval module.

About this briefing: an overview of the aims of the COBRE-S Sustainability Phase, what is changing, the program's mission, and how applications will be reviewed. The exact application components are covered on each contributor's page.

4 · Eligibility: Center Director (PD/PI)

What the NOFO requires and expects of the COBRE PD/PI (the Center Director).
Items marked “at submission” must already be in place when the COBRE-S application is submitted.

RequirementDetail
ExperienceExpected to be an established biomedical research scientist with the administrative and mentoring experience needed to lead the Center.
Continuing from Phase 2A COBRE Phase 2 PD/PI moving into COBRE-S may continue in the role if their scientific background falls within the breadth of the proposed area. Alternatively, the institution may propose a new PD/PI.
Appointment (at submission)Primary appointment at the applicant organization.
Funding (at submission)PD/PI on at least one active, peer-reviewed, externally funded research grant within the general scientific area of the COBRE. If that grant is a multi-PD/PI award to a different organization, the applicant organization must receive the PD/PI's research funds via a subcontract.
Multiple PD/PIsIf the application has multiple PD/PIs, each non-contact PD/PI must also hold at least one active, peer-reviewed, externally funded research grant in the COBRE's general scientific area.
One IDeA award at a timeMay not simultaneously lead more than one IDeA program award (INBRE, CTR, or another COBRE).
Prior COBRE leadershipPrevious PD/PIs of a different COBRE Center are not eligible to lead a COBRE-E or COBRE-S application (except those who served only on an interim basis).
EffortAt least 3 person-months for administrative oversight of the Center and career guidance for investigators. A maximum of 6 person-months of salary may be requested for all PD/PIs combined.
No research supportMay not use COBRE funds for their own laboratory research and is not eligible for research project support from this or any other COBRE, INBRE, or CTR award.
Evidence of mentoring and leadershipThe biosketch Personal Statement must describe administrative and mentoring experience, including leadership in the Center's research area, mentoring of early- and mid-career investigators, and administration (such as directing a previous phase of a COBRE award).
National meetingMust attend the biennial National IDeA Symposium of Biomedical Research Excellence (NISBRE).

5 · Unchanged Rules: Expired Phase 2 NOFO to New COBRE-E/S NOFO
RuleDetail
RPG application deadlineBy the end of Year 2 of COBRE support. New application: ESIs, NIs, “at-risk” investigators. Renewal: mid-career, “at-risk” investigators.
Length of supportExpected maximum of 3 years, then transition to independent support. Beyond 3 years requires AC approval and prior NIGMS approval.
Minimum effort6.0 person-months for the duration of COBRE support.
Independent fundingSI is no longer eligible for COBRE support upon receipt of independent research funding (any substantial, independent research award, such as an R01 or R01-equivalent, from NIH or another agency).
Other IDeA awardsSI cannot receive simultaneous research support from any other IDeA program award, but may serve as project lead of a supplement to an IDeA award.
RemovalPossible if AC review finds inadequate progress toward milestones, including failure to submit an RPG application by the end of two years.
