Speaker notes for this slide

Key changes in brief
  • No new cores. Consolidating existing cores, and sharing facilities supported by other IDeA programs, is strongly encouraged.
  • Scientific Research Program (SRP) is a required component. It now also coordinates mentoring and career development.
  • Clinical trials are not allowed. Human subjects activities that are not clinical trials are allowed.
  • The Data Management and Sharing (DMS) Plan goes in the Overall component.
  • New SIs, starting research: without human subjects or vertebrate animals, notify NIGMS 14+ days ahead (no prior approval needed). With human subjects or vertebrate animals, NIGMS prior approval is required, submitted through the eRA Prior Approval module 30+ days ahead (NOT-OD-26-026).

NOFO numbers used in this briefing
  • PAR-27-055: COBRE-E/S NOFO (Expansion and Sustainability). This application (S phase). Current; expires May 26, 2029.
  • PAR-23-063: previous COBRE Phase 2 NOFO; reissued as PAR-27-055.
  • PAR-23-216: previous COBRE Phase 3 (Transitional Centers) NOFO; expired October 1, 2025. Compared in Section 3.
  • NOT-OD-26-026: NIH notice (not a NOFO) on prior approval requests through the eRA Prior Approval module.
